# Cambodia eligibility boundary

Last reviewed: 2026-10-30. This is a product-safety record, not legal advice or a
determination that any person may use perpetual futures.

The application deliberately labels Cambodia futures eligibility **unverified**.
Public market-data access, a successful website visit, contract discovery, or the
2022 SERC/Binance memorandum cannot establish user/account eligibility.

Official material reviewed:

- The [SERC announcement of the 30 June 2022 Binance memorandum](https://www.serc.gov.kh/boards/index.php?bid=m77annountment&cate=&nav=read&no=275&ntcmode=read&p=32&sch=&sch_key=&sch_type=)
  describes technical/regulatory cooperation and stated at that date that SERC had
  not issued a digital-asset licence. A memorandum is not an operating licence.
- The [13 July 2018 NBC joint-statement page](https://www.nbc.gov.kh/english/news_and_events/news_info.php?id=380)
  warned about unlicensed cryptocurrency activity. Its age means it must not be
  treated as a complete statement of current law.
- SERC publishes a current [list of derivative brokers and approved products](https://www.serc.gov.kh/english/m67.php).
  That list concerns the Cambodian regulated derivatives framework and does not by
  itself establish that a Binance USDⓈ-M account or product is authorised for this
  user.

Before any manual futures activity, the operator must independently obtain current,
account-specific confirmation from Binance's official eligibility/terms/support flow
and, where appropriate, qualified Cambodian counsel or the competent regulator.
The software never bypasses a 403/451, alternate-host restriction or account control.
