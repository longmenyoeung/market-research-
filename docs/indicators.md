# Indicator methods

Generated by `backend/scripts/export_indicator_docs.py` from the calculation catalog. This document contains formulas and default parameters, never live prices.

All inputs are confirmed candles in ascending UTC order, with no interpolated gaps. Warm-up is the minimum number of input bars before a value may be returned; zero denominators, insufficient history or missing inputs can keep a value unavailable longer. EMA and iterative indicators depend on the loaded history and their stated seed. Open candles are chart-only until confirmed.

Default units: prices in USDT, volume in base-contract units, oscillator values in their stated scale, volatility in annualized percent on a 365-day calendar. Evidence uses five capped groups (trend, structure, momentum, volume, context). Descriptive indicators within one group do not create independent confirmations.

## Derived observations

Volume spike: latest relative volume >=2. Price/volume disagreement: sign of the 20-observation close change opposes the OBV change over the same observations. Volatility percentile uses the trailing sample, assigns half of ties to each side, and is compressed at <=20 or elevated at >=80. Scanner volatility warning separately uses configurable ATR/price (default 3%). Regime is trending at ADX>=25, otherwise ranging.

VWAP resets at UTC midnight, using typical price (H+L+C)/3 and candle volume. The first loaded UTC day may be partial; this is not an exchange trade-by-trade VWAP.

True volume profile is unavailable until reliable trade-at-price data is collected. Candle volume is never distributed over invented prices. See [drawing rules](drawing-rules.md) for Fibonacci, floor pivots, prior periods, support/resistance, price channels, patterns and divergence.
